1. Executive Summary

Duolingo's crossover into Brawl Stars, and its reactive, gamified marketing during the 2026 World Cup (Duo Cup), proved that Duolingo grows fastest by borrowing attention from platforms people already use daily — not by asking users to carve out separate "study time."

This PRD proposes the next channel in that playbook: Spotify. Instead of a limited-time crossover event, this is a persistent, always-on feature: a lightweight lesson generated from the lyrics of songs users are already streaming, surfaced via a native in-app widget, with Duolingo returning engaged learners to Spotify through a cross-app reward loop.

Where Brawl Stars proved novelty-driven acquisition and Duo Cup proved gamified retention, Spotify is chosen to prove habit-stacking — attaching language practice to something users already do every day, at a frequency neither prior partnership could sustain.

2. Strategic Alignment: Why Spotify & Why Now?

Expanding Duolingo from isolated study time to an always-on daily lifestyle habit.

The Problem We're SolvingWhy This Partnership Wins
People listen without understanding. Millions stream songs in Spanish, Korean, or French every day, but have no idea what the lyrics mean. That's a huge group of interested learners sitting right in front of us. Tapping into a massive daily audience. Spotify has over 600 million daily listeners who are already playing this music. We don't have to find new users — they are already there.
Isolated study time causes churn. Opening a separate app just to "study" feels like homework. When life gets busy, study habits are the first thing people drop. Building on existing habits (habit-stacking). Instead of asking people to make time for Duolingo, we attach 2-minute lessons to things they already do daily — commuting, walking, working out.
"This builds on a pattern Duolingo already runs — from Brawl Stars to World Cup marketing."

3. User Personas & Intent Triggers

The Lyric-Curious Listener

Spotify-primary · No Duolingo account

Streams non-English music daily (K-pop, reggaeton, Afrobeats). Wants to know what a favorite song means but has no easy way to find out mid-listen.

Intent trigger: hearing a song on repeat without understanding the lyrics

The Lapsed Learner

Has a Duolingo account · Inactive 30+ days

Already has language intent and prior investment, but the standalone-app habit didn't stick.

Intent trigger: encountering a song in their target language, re-surfacing dormant motivation

The Active Streaker

Active Duolingo user · High streak

Already motivated; the value here is retention and delight — turning passive commute time into an extra practice rep without feeling like more homework.

Intent trigger: wanting an easy, low-effort way to protect today's streak

4. End-to-End User Journey

How seamless cross-app integration turns a Spotify listening session into a Duolingo lesson.

1

Spotify CTA

  • Targeted trigger: a small "Learn this song" chip appears on the lyrics view for supported non-English tracks.
  • User eligibility: shown to users with active or expired Duolingo accounts, or those who opt in via a one-time prompt.
  • Action: tapping the chip initiates the cross-app learning handoff.
2

Deep Link Handoff

  • Data transfer: firing the chip sends song ID, artist, and detected-language payload into the Duolingo app.
  • Edge-case handling: directs to the installed Duolingo app, or a lightweight web view for users without it.
  • Frictionless entry: opens immediately, no manual setup or searching.
3

Duolingo Lesson

  • Custom content: opens directly to a 2–3 minute interactive lesson built from that song's lyrics.
  • Key exercises: vocabulary flashcards from key phrases and lyric fill-in-the-blank drills.
  • Comprehension check: ends with a quick check to reinforce newly learned song vocabulary.
4

Reward Loop

  • Duolingo side: awards XP and preserves/extends streak credit upon lesson completion.
  • Spotify side: unlocks a "Song Unlocked" badge displayed back on that track's lyrics page.
  • Growth loop: closes the loop by driving repeat listens with full lyric understanding.

5. Core Feature Specifications (MVP Scope)

Feature 1 — CEFR Lyric Parsing & Song Selection

Mechanism & rules:

  • Automated classification: maps Spotify lyrics to standard CEFR levels (A1–C2) based on vocabulary frequency and syntax complexity.
  • Smart filtering: hides prompts for tracks exceeding the learner's current level, to prevent frustration.
  • Safety & quality pipeline: filters out explicit tracks and heavy slang in v1 to protect brand reputation.
  • Template pre-processing: pre-builds 2-minute lesson templates (5–8 key terms + 1 grammar pattern).

Success metrics: Classification precision ≥ 95% alignment with CEFR standards · Zero explicit-content leaks in auto-generated lessons · Modal conversion rate (% who click "Start Learning This Song").

Feature 2 — Contextual In-App Discovery (Spotify Widget)

Mechanism & rules:

  • Dynamic widget injection: embeds a native "Learn this song with Duolingo" prompt directly inside the Spotify player view for foreign-language tracks.
  • Targeted triggering: displays dynamically based on detected song language and Duolingo profile status.
  • One-tap entry: tapping the widget initiates the deep-linking sequence straight into the lesson.

Success metrics: Widget CTR (% of listeners who tap the chip) · Top-of-funnel acquisition (new Duolingo sign-ups initiated from Spotify).

Feature 3 — Interactive Lyric Micro-Lessons (Duolingo App)

Mechanism & rules:

  • Audio-anchored drills: plays a 5-second audio snippet of the actual track alongside fill-in-the-blank vocabulary challenges.
  • Real-time pedagogical feedback: provides immediate translation breakdowns and grammar explanations (e.g. "denke = I think").
  • Bite-sized pacing: designed to take under 120 seconds, awarding XP and preserving daily streaks.

Success metrics: Lesson completion rate (% of started song lessons finished) · D1 retention uplift (lift in next-day app open rate for users completing song drills).

Content pipeline (offline/batch)Ingests lyrics + metadata → CEFR classifier → safety filter → template-based lesson generator → human review queue → published lesson store

Cross-app signal serviceOn lesson completion, fires a webhook to Spotify to unlock the "Song Unlocked" badge; stores anonymized completion data for both teams' analytics

10. Risks & Mitigations

RiskMitigation

Explicit or culturally sensitive lyrics used in lessonsExplicit-content flag from Spotify's metadata excludes tracks from the auto-pipeline by default

Deep link fails on older app versionsWeb-based fallback lesson requiring no app update
Feature only appeals to a narrow slice of catalog (non-English tracks)Tracked as a targeted acquisition/retention channel, judged against its own funnel — not overall DAU

11. Monetization & Business Model

  • No direct revenue share in v1 — modeled as a mutual growth partnership, matching the Brawl Stars precedent, which was a brand/audience exchange rather than a paid placement.
  • Duolingo upside: new and reactivated users who convert to Super Duolingo downstream — tracked via a 90-day cohort conversion rate versus standard acquisition channels.
  • Spotify upside: a differentiated lyrics-page engagement feature that indirectly supports ad impressions and Premium retention.
  • Future paid extension (Could-Have): co-branded seasonal campaigns or sponsored artist-specific lesson packs tied to album releases.
